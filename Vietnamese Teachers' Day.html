Dear Teacher,

Today, we pause to say a heartfelt thank you. You do far more than teach lessons: you spark curiosity, build confidence and believe in us even when we doubt ourselves.

Your patience, kindness and dedication stay with us long after the school bell rings. Wishing you good health, joy and happiness today and every day.

A good teacher can inspire hope, ignite the imagination and instill a love of learning.

GratitudeThank you for every lesson, big and small.
RespectHonoring the dedication behind every classroom.
GrowthBecause of you, we keep learning and growing.

With love and gratitude on Vietnamese Teachers' Day
